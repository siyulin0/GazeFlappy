How it works

Your gaze becomes a flight path

Webcam→Gaze estimate→Smoothing→Bird

WebGazer estimates where you are looking using video processed in your browser. We smooth the vertical estimate and let the bird gradually follow it—there is no click-to-flap physics.

Gaze Flappy is an experimental visual-break game that encourages users to vary their gaze direction after prolonged screen work. It is not a medical device.
