    How it works

    Pick how you want to fly

    Gaze Mode

    Webcam→Look up or down→Bird follows

    Look higher on the screen to fly up, or lower to fly down. Your bird follows your gaze smoothly while moving forward through the course.

    Blink Mode

    Webcam→Blink→Bird flaps

    Blink both eyes normally to flap upward. The bird falls between blinks, so time each flap to slip through the gaps.

    Everything from your camera is processed in your browser. SightFlight is an experimental game, not a medical device.
